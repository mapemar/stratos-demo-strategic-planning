Structuring / Institutional toolkit

IFC product & partnership matching

A decision-support guide to identify possible instruments and World Bank Group coordination routes. Final product selection belongs to IFC investment teams.
01 · Direct investment

Loans & equity

For commercially viable private-sector projects and companies where long-term financing, risk sharing or capital is needed.

  • Project and corporate finance
  • Equity and quasi-equity
  • Local-currency solutions where available
Potential fit: bankable project / company
02 · Mobilisation

Syndications & co-investment

Bring additional lenders and investors alongside IFC to increase financing capacity and crowd in private capital.

  • B-loans and parallel loans
  • Managed co-lending structures
  • Institutional investor partnerships
Potential fit: larger financing requirement
03 · Risk mitigation

Guarantees & risk sharing

Support financing by addressing specific risks, subject to the applicable product, eligibility and approval framework.

  • Portfolio and transaction risk sharing
  • Trade and supply-chain finance
  • Coordination with MIGA where relevant
Potential fit: identifiable risk constraint
04 · Advisory

Upstream & advisory

Help address policy, regulatory, market and project-preparation barriers that prevent investment from reaching financial close.

  • Market creation and sector reform
  • PPP and transaction advisory
  • Environmental and social standards
Potential fit: market or project readiness gap
05 · Public-sector interface

IBRD / World Bank coordination

Identify where sovereign, policy or public infrastructure issues require coordination with the World Bank rather than an IFC-only solution.

  • Policy and regulatory constraints
  • Public infrastructure enablers
  • Complementary private-sector investment
Potential fit: public-sector dependency
06 · Political risk

MIGA coordination

Consider political risk insurance and guarantees for eligible cross-border investment risks, in coordination with the relevant teams.

  • Transfer and convertibility
  • Expropriation and breach of contract
  • War and civil disturbance
Potential fit: eligible political risk
Product descriptions are high-level orientation, not legal or product eligibility advice. Current terms and approval requirements must be checked against official IFC / MIGA documentation.
